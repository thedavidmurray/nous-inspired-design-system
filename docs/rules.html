Rulebook

Nine rules that survived thirty rounds and about two thousand prompts. Each rule is one sentence and three copyable prompts, cited by tag id into docs/prompts/exploration-log.txt. Proof images attach one keeper at a time, with the prompt captured at harvest.

R1One ink, then restraint

Pick one ink and commit: Prussian blue cyanotype on cream, phosphor green on near-black, or black on foxed cream with a single small red mark. Never navy plus gold, never rainbow by default. Color is the minority lane, not the house style.

R2Texture is the medium

Every image goes through a print or early-digital process: risograph misregistration, halftone rosette, Bayer dither, thermal-receipt grain, xerox degradation, CRT scanlines. Clean renders read as stock. When it looks too smooth, add one texture clause.

R3Statuary in, living faces out

No photographic or living human faces. Classical marble statuary, busts, reliefs, and caryatids are approved as art objects: gods in the machine, glitched into dither. Machine habitat, not portraiture.

R4Only the real lexicon

Text stays anchored to words Nous actually uses: NOUS RESEARCH and HERMES first, project names rarely. Latin letters only, no invented slogans, no Greek script. Match the word to the subject: a bazaar can say open source, a cathedral cannot.

R6The spectral break

The deliberate break from print texture is false-color sensor imagery: FLIR thermal, Aerochrome infrared, UV fluorescence, X-ray, night vision, schlieren, lidar. Wordmarks become HUD labels and scale bars. Moodboard grain reads as sensor noise here, so keep it on.

R8Discovered styles: technique transfers, atmosphere swallows

Roll --sref random to discover styles, then read the seed off the job card. Seeds that encode rendering technique (linework, palette, print process) transfer to any subject and keep the wordmark. Seeds that encode atmosphere erase subject and wordmark; use them only on the off-brief lane.

R9The Nous Girl rides as an image prompt

On 8.2 her reference goes first in the prompt as a bare image URL with --iw 1.5. Identity holds, palette survives, the scene survives only if the subject is not itself a figure: pair her with architecture, instruments, and machines, never statuary. --oref and --cref are refused; --iw 3 collapses to monochrome portraits.
